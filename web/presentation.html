Market Opportunity The Failure of the Informal Sector

Why the "Word-of-Mouth" Sector Fails Everyone

9 out of 10 urban households find domestic help through security guards or random neighbor referrals. This creates a lose-lose dynamic.

Customer Pain Points

  • ❌ Sudden No-Shows & Ghosting: Maids take 4-5 days off with zero notice, wrecking morning work routines.
  • ❌ Safety & Trust Anxieties: No Aadhaar biometric check, criminal verification, or theft recourse.
  • ❌ Awkward Wage Haggling: Arbitrary pricing with zero clarity on exact chores or standard leave policies.

Domestic Worker Pain Points

  • ⚠️ Income Instability: Arbitrary wage cuts, delayed payments, and zero health or accident insurance.
  • ⚠️ High Transit Friction: Traveling 5-10 km across the city between 1-hour jobs drains time and money.
  • ⚠️ Zero Dignity & Exploitation: No defined work hours, no rest breaks, and verbal mistreatment without support.
🎯 MaidPro Mission: Build a dignified, reliable bridge offering institutional trust, fair wages, and a guaranteed 24-hr replacement SLA.

Expansion & Scaling Hyperlocal Density Playbook

The Cluster Density Flywheel: Scaling 0 to 1 & City to City

Scaling domestic help is an exercise in **geographic density**, not widespread marketing.

01

Micro-Cluster Infiltration

Target a tight 1–2 km radius with 1,500+ premium apartments (e.g., Kamla Nagar & Dayalbagh in Agra). Maximize local density so travel times stay under 10 minutes.

02

The Trojan Horse Entry Hook

Acquire homes via subsidized ₹399–₹999 deep cleans (kitchen chimney, bathroom tiles). Once inside the home, cross-sell recurring monthly housekeeping.

03

The Hub-and-Spoke Launch

Scale to new cities (Noida, Lucknow, Jaipur) using a centralized remote CRM + 1 local Ops Cell (1 City Lead + 2 Field Coordinators + NGO hiring tie-ups).

🚦 City Expansion Gating Metric: Do not unlock a new city until worker shift density > 75% and replacement SLA < 24 hrs.

Risk Mitigation Building an Enduring Moat

Neutralizing the 3 Major Industry Traps

Why standard agencies fail, and how MaidPro guarantees operational stability.

Trap 1: Leakage / Bypass

Clients Cutting Out Platform

The Threat: Client deals directly with maid after month 1.
The Fix: If the maid leaves or falls sick, off-platform clients are stranded. Active MaidPro members receive free replacements within 24 hours + backup maid on leave days.
Trap 2: No-Shows & Churn

Sudden Unannounced Absences

The Threat: Worker misses morning shift with no warning.
The Fix: 10% Floating Reserve ("Standby Fleet") per cluster on a guaranteed daily stipend, ready to deploy emergency replacements in under 45 mins.
Trap 3: Safety & Theft Claims

Property Disputes & Trust

The Threat: Accidental breakage or theft destroys brand name.
The Fix: 100% Aadhaar biometric & local police verification + MaidPro In-Home Fidelity Insurance protecting up to ₹50,000 property damage.
